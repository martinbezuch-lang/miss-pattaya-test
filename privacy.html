Privacy Policy

MISS of Pattaya (“we”, “the site”) is an online popularity contest where visitors vote for their favourite contestants. This page explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account data – your e-mail address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and e-mail address that Google shares with us. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access any other Google data (no contacts, Drive, Gmail etc.).
  • Profile data you enter yourself – for example gender, year of birth, country and optional answers such as favourite food or city.
  • Contestants only – photos, first name, age, country, social media links and contact details (WhatsApp / LINE) that the contestant provides, and an identity document with a selfie used only to verify that she is a real adult woman. Identity documents are stored in a private storage area that only the organisers can open; they are never shown on the site.
  • Votes – which contestant you voted for and when, to enforce the voting limits.
  • Visit statistics – which parts of the site are opened, the language, the country, whether the device is a phone, and an anonymous fingerprint of the IP address (a one-way hash; the IP address itself is not stored).
  • Push notifications – if you turn them on, your browser’s push subscription so we can send contest notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your browser.

How we use it

  • to let you sign in, vote and manage your profile;
  • to run the contest fairly (vote limits, preventing fake accounts and abuse);
  • to verify contestants’ identity and age (18+) and to contact contestants about the contest and prizes;
  • to send notifications you have turned on;
  • to understand how the site is used and improve it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. Data received from Google is used only for signing in, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What is public

A contestant’s photos, first name, age, country, the optional profile answers she chooses to fill in, her social media links and her vote count are visible to everyone. Voters’ names and e-mail addresses are never shown publicly.

Services we use

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and file storage (servers in the EU, Ireland);
  • GitHub Pages – hosting of the website;
  • Google – “Sign in with Google”, only if you choose it;
  • GeoJS – detecting the visitor’s country from the IP address for statistics and regional settings;
  • browser push services (Apple, Google, Mozilla) – delivery of notifications you have turned on.

Storage on your device

The site stores your sign-in session, language, theme and similar settings in your browser’s local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep data

Account and profile data are kept while your account exists. Identity documents are kept only as long as needed for verification and the contest. Visit statistics are kept in aggregated, anonymous form.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, by e-mail. Contestants can ask to have their profile and photos removed at any time.

Terms of Use

  • MISS of Pattaya is an online popularity contest. Winners are decided only by votes – there is no lottery or draw.
  • Contestants must be women aged 18 or over and must pass identity verification before they can receive a prize. Prizes are described on the site under “Prizes”.
  • Contestants confirm that they own the photos they upload and agree to their publication on the site.
  • One person may use one account. Votes from fake or automated accounts, bots or any attempt to manipulate the results may be removed and the accounts blocked.
  • The organisers may remove content or contestants that break these terms, the law or common decency (no nudity or sexual content).
  • Results shown on the site are indicative until confirmed by the organisers.
  • The site is provided “as is”. We may change the contest rules or these terms; the current version is always on this page.
